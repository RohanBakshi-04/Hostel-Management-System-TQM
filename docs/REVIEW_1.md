> *"Based on the last digit of my roll number (`7`), my assigned baseline software is the **Hostel Management System**, and my sequentially assigned Quality Goal is **Q05: Reduce Response Time**."*
>
> *"In university hostels, administrators handle heavy workloads during admission and semester check-ins. Under TQM principles, reducing operational response time directly improves customer satisfaction and administrative productivity."*

### 2.3 Proposed System Architecture & Q05 Quality Strategy
> *"To solve latency bottlenecks, I have architected a **Layered Service-Repository System** comprising:*
> 1. *Presentation Layer (Responsive Tkinter / TTK Desktop GUI with persistent in-memory frame switching)*
> 2. *Service & Business Validation Layer (Poka-Yoke error prevention on student/room forms)*
> 3. *Q05 Performance Optimization Layer (Fast Search, Quick Navigation, Cached Dashboard, B-Tree Database Indexing, Paginated Reports)*
> 4. *Persistence Layer (SQLite3 in Write-Ahead Logging mode with relational foreign key enforcement)*
